# YouTube Music Enhancer

**A cleaner, more customizable and powerful YouTube Music experience.**

YouTube Music Enhancer is a lightweight browser extension that improves the **YouTube Music player UI** with a cleaner layout, easier video quality control, a Spotify-inspired bottom player, customizable accent colors, keyboard shortcuts, and an optional distraction-free player mode.

## Features

### Video Quality Control

Quickly change YouTube Music video quality directly from the player.

Supported quality options:

* Auto
* 4K (2160p)
* 1440p
* 1080p
* 720p
* 480p
* 360p

The extension automatically hides quality options that are not available for the current video.

### Spotify-Style Player Layout

The bottom player is reorganized for a cleaner and more familiar experience:

* Song information on the left
* Playback controls centered
* Volume and additional controls on the right
* Better use of available screen space
* Cleaner and more modern player experience

### Distraction-Free Player

Hide the right-side panels and give the music video more space.

Perfect for users who want a **clean YouTube Music interface without unnecessary panels**.

### Keyboard Shortcuts

Control important features without reaching for your mouse.

| Shortcut  | Action                      |
| --------- | --------------------------- |
| `Alt + P` | Show / Hide Side Panel      |
| `Alt + Q` | Open Video Quality Selector |

### Custom Accent Color

Personalize the extension with your preferred accent color.

Your selected settings are saved locally, so your preferences persist between sessions.

### Lightweight & Non-Intrusive

The extension focuses on improving the existing YouTube Music interface rather than replacing it.

It works by enhancing the current player UI with additional controls and styling.

## Installation

### Chrome / Chromium-based browsers

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open your browser's extension page.
3. Enable **Developer Mode**.
4. Select **Load unpacked**.
5. Choose the extension directory.
6. Open **YouTube Music** and start listening.

## Usage

After installation, open **YouTube Music**.

The extension adds additional controls to the player interface.

Use:

* **Quality** to change video quality
* **Hide / Show** to toggle the side panel
* **Alt + Q** for quick quality selection
* **Alt + P** for quick panel control

Your quality and accent color preferences are stored locally using browser storage.

## Privacy

YouTube Music Enhancer is designed to work directly on the YouTube Music interface.

User preferences such as:

* Video quality
* Accent color

are stored locally in the browser.

No account or external service is required for these settings.
